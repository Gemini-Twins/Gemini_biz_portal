Registration Pathway Steps

Check off each step as you complete it on Google.

1

Sign In to Google Business Profile

Access the portal using your primary business Google account.

Go to the official Google Business Profile website and sign in with the Google Account you wish to use to manage your business listing.

Open Google Business Portal
2

Enter Your Business Name

Search to check if your business already exists or create new.

Type in your exact business name. If it appears in the dropdown, claim it. If not, click "Add your business to Google".

3

Choose Business Category

Select the category that best describes your core services.

Select your primary business category carefully. This helps Google match your business with local customer searches. You can add secondary categories later.

4

Add Location & Service Areas

Specify whether customers can visit your physical address.

Choose if you have a physical storefront customers can visit. If you provide services at customer locations instead, select service areas.

5

Add Contact Info & Website

Provide phone number and website link for customer inquiries.

Enter your active phone number and website URL so clients can easily reach out or visit your online store.

6

Verify Your Business

Confirm ownership via phone, email, or video verification.

Google will require verification to prove you own the business. Options usually include SMS code, phone call, email, or recording a video of your workspace/signage.
